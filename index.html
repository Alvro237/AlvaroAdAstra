About me

Per aspera ad astra

I just graduated from my bachelor in aerospace engineering, so I am officially an engineer. My college years were the most complicated yet fascinating of my life. Each challenge brought the best of me and my classmates, and the lessons learned will follow me throughout my career.

You may wonder where it leads. The name of this page gives a hint: "Through hardships to the stars." This journey started when I decided to become an astronaut, over 15 years ago. Over time I've met brilliant minds, discovered new branches of engineering, and my reasons to pursue this career have evolved. Now, the will to contribute to humanity's knowledge and secure our place among the stars drives me forward.

I am not an astronaut yet. But my passion for engineering is still there: rocket propulsion, astrophysics, satellites, launches, space vehicles, and programming. Because no matter how incredible the destination, the aerospace journey still fascinates me every single day.
